# Low-Energy & ADHD-Friendly Daily Routine Guide

A beginner-friendly manual and printable static site for low-energy days and ADHD-friendly routines. Pick one task at a time, follow gentle schedules, rest without guilt, and track your energy — no guilt, no hustle, just small steps.

## What's inside

- **docs/** — the beginner manual in Markdown: one-task-at-a-time, gentle schedules, rest without guilt, energy tracking, a low-energy task list, and printable routine cards.
- **site/** — a static HTML version of the guide you can open in any browser or host anywhere. Includes printable routine cards designed to print cleanly from your browser.

## Who this is for

Anyone who struggles to start or finish daily tasks when energy is low — especially ADHD brains, chronic illness, burnout, or just a hard day. Written with a kind, small-steps approach.
