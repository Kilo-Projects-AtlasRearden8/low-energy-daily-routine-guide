# Low-Energy & ADHD-Friendly Daily Routine Guide

A beginner-friendly manual and printable static site for low-energy and ADHD-friendly daily routines. Pick one task at a time, follow gentle schedules, rest without guilt, and track your energy — no guilt, no hustle, just small steps.

## Who this is for

Anyone who struggles to start or finish daily tasks when energy is low — especially ADHD brains, chronic illness, burnout, or just a hard day. Written with a kind, small-steps approach. You do not need to do everything here. Pick one idea, try it for a day or two, and drop it if it does not help.

## How to use it

1. Open `site/index.html` in any web browser. No server, no install, no internet needed.
2. Read one section at a time. Jump around. Skip what does not help.
3. For printable cards, open `site/cards.html`, press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P), enable background graphics, and cut along the card borders.

Optional local preview:
```
cd site
python3 -m http.server 8000
# then open http://localhost:8000
```

## The golden rules

- **One task at a time.** Not three. One.
- **Small steps count.** Brushing your teeth is a win.
- **Rest is part of the routine.** Not something you earn.
- **A hard day does not break a streak.** There is no streak.
- **This is not medical advice.** Talk to a clinician about health concerns.
