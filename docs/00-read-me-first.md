# Low-Energy & ADHD-Friendly Daily Routine Guide

Welcome. This is a beginner manual for days when everything feels like too much. It was written by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — a small business focused on beginner-friendly guides and practical tools — with a kind, small-steps approach.

## Who this is for

- You have ADHD and starting a task feels impossible
- You are low on energy from illness, burnout, stress, or just life
- You want a routine that does not guilt-trip you
- You have never used a planner before, or every planner you tried felt like a chore

## How to use this guide

Read one section at a time. That is the whole method. You do not need to do everything here. You do not even need to do most of it. Pick one idea, try it for a day or two, and drop it if it does not help.

Each section stands alone. Jump around. Skip sections. Come back later.

## The golden rules

- **One task at a time.** Not three. Not "after this, that." One.
- **Small steps count.** Brushing your teeth is a win. Drinking water is a win.
- **Rest is part of the routine, not a reward you have to earn.**
- **If today is a wash, tomorrow is a new day.** No streak to break, no points to lose.
- **This is not medical advice.** If you are struggling with your health, talk to a clinician.
