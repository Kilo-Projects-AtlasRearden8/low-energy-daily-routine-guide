# Energy Tracking

Track your energy so you can plan with reality instead of wishes.

## The idea

Energy changes day to day, and hour to hour. Tracking it helps you match tasks to the energy you actually have, instead of beating yourself up for not having more.

## The 1–5 energy scale

Rate your energy a few times a day (morning, midday, evening):

- **5** — I can do several things and start new ones
- **4** — I can do one or two tasks with breaks
- **3** — I can do one small task, then rest
- **2** — I can do basic self-care (water, food, restroom)
- **1** — I need rest. Doing nothing is the right choice

## Simple tracking sheet

Copy this into a notebook or your phone:

| Day | Morning | Midday | Evening | What helped | What drained |
|-----|---------|--------|---------|-------------|--------------|
| Mon | 2 | 2 | 1 | Nap, quiet | Email, noise |
| Tue | 4 | 3 | 3 | Walk outside | Multitasking |

You do not need to fill every row. One row a day is plenty.

## How to use your tracking

- **Plan low first.** Schedule your hardest task for your highest-energy block, or skip it entirely on 1–2 days.
- **Spot patterns.** Do you crash after 3:00 PM? Protect that time for rest.
- **Trust the low numbers.** A "1" day is not a failed day. It is a rest day.
