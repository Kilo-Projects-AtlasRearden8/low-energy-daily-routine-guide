# Rest Without Guilt

Rest is not a reward for finishing your to-do list. Rest is part of being a person.

## The idea

On low-energy days, resting *is* the productive thing. Pushing through exhaustion usually costs more than it gains. You do not have to earn permission to sit down.

## What guilt-free rest looks like

- Lying down in the middle of the day because your body asked for it
- Saying "I am not doing anything else today" without explaining why
- Taking a nap and not counting it against yourself
- Watching a show, reading, or staring out a window with no goal

## How to rest without the guilt spiral

1. **Name it.** Say out loud: "I am resting now." Naming it makes it a choice, not a collapse.
2. **Set a loose container.** "I will rest for 20 minutes" — or "I will rest until I feel ready." Either is fine.
3. **Do not bargain.** Do not say "I can rest after I finish the dishes." If you need rest, rest first.
4. **Notice what restored you.** Did a nap help? Did silence help? Keep what works, drop what does not.

## ADHD and rest

ADHD brains often run on interest and urgency, which makes rest feel unsafe — like you will forget everything or fall behind. Rest will still be there when you get up. The dishes will still be there too, and they can wait.
