# Low-Energy Task List

A starter list of tiny tasks for when you cannot think of anything to do. Pick one. Or pick none. Both are fine.

## Self-care (2 minutes or less)

- Drink a glass of water
- Wash your face
- Put on clean socks
- Step outside for one minute
- Take three slow breaths
- Brush your teeth (even just the front ones)

## Around the home (tiny versions)

- Put one dish in the dishwasher
- Fold one piece of laundry
- Throw away one piece of trash
- Put one item where it belongs
- Wipe one counter
- Open one curtain or window

## Movement

- Stretch for 30 seconds
- Walk to the end of the block and back
- Stand up and sit down three times
- Roll your shoulders

## Rest (counts as a task)

- Lie down for 10 minutes
- Close your eyes and listen to one song
- Sit in a chair with a blanket
- Do nothing for five minutes

## Make your own list

Write 5–10 tasks that feel easy *for you* on a low day. Keep this list where you can see it. When energy is low, choose from the list instead of deciding from scratch.

**Rule:** If a task on your list ever feels too big, make it smaller. "Wash dishes" becomes "rinse one spoon." That still counts.
